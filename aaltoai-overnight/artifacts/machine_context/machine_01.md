# Unit 4 - north line skid

## What this unit is

Unit 4 is the older of the two skids on this line, commissioned 2019.
Channel 21 sits on the recirculation loop and is the one operators watch first.

## Normal behaviour

Channel 21 rests near 94 and dips briefly on every changeover.
On a busy shift a changeover happens every 40 samples or so, and the dips are expected.

## Known history

- August: repeated dips on Channel 21 were traced to a sticking valve on the loop.
  The valve was replaced and the dips stopped for three weeks.
- The same pattern came back last week. The valve is the first thing to check.

### sim1_run16_ev01
Abrupt SPE anomaly detected from sample 256 to 282, driven primarily by col_021 dropping below normal operation.
Channels col_009 and col_006 showed leading co-movement with the leader.
Operators concluded the recurrence is tied to the recirculation loop valve.

### sim194_run27_ev18
Abrupt SPE anomaly observed from sample 840 to 862, primarily driven by col_021 dropping below normal operation with an attribution share of 0.7731.
Channels col_009 and col_002 exhibited leading co-movement with the leader, and operators concluded it matches the recirculation loop valve pattern.
